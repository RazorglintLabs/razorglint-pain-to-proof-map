# Pilot Scope Boundaries

What each pilot level includes, excludes, and where the upgrade trigger lives.

---

## 1. Cheap Discovery Call / Free Review

### What buyer gets
- Pain naming: all 26 painpoints explained in plain language
- Public maps: PAINPOINT_INDEX, SYSTEM_SOLUTION_MAP, PILOT_STACK_MAP
- Claim boundary education: what we can and cannot say
- Tier guidance: which systems map to their pain

### What buyer does NOT get
- Solution-path depth for any system
- Proof bundles or receipts
- Integration logic or architecture detail
- Custom Truthpacks
- Governed-agent demonstrations
- Simulation access

### Painpoints allowed
- All 26 may be named and explained at surface level
- PP-013, PP-014 may be explored in depth (sales enablement / buying path)

### Painpoints out of scope
- All S1 Critical solution paths
- All system integration depth

### Upgrade trigger
Buyer asks "how does this actually work?" or "can you show me a receipt/proof?"

### What buyer gets
- Negotiated module selection and integration scope
- White-label or embedded deployment rights
- Custom integration support
- Exclusivity terms (geography, vertical, or use case)

### What buyer does NOT get

- IP transfer (unless Private IP Sale)

### Painpoints allowed
Negotiated per deal — any combination at any depth

### Upgrade trigger
Buyer asks about full IP acquisition or platform-level ownership

## Pilot spine systems

All pilots that include system demonstrations use the pilot spine:

1. **SAR Guardian** — enforcement / receipt backbone (Professional+)
2. **OpenClaw Governed Agents** — governed AI-agent surface (Enterprise)
3. **FleetSim Trust Plane** — simulation-grade fleet trust (Operator+)

These three systems are the primary demonstration surface. Supporting systems (Blackiron, IronSeal, Voidlock, ChessClock, PolicySourceEngine, Command Guardian) provide evidence infrastructure visible at their respective tier boundaries.

## Non-claim boundary

Pilot scope boundaries define what is demonstrated, not what is technically possible. All systems remain independently testable in their own repos. Pilots show governed behavior on scoped paths — they do not deliver production integration, guarantee outcomes, or replace buyer due diligence.
