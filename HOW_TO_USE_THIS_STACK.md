# How to Use This Stack

Five usage paths depending on who you are and what you need.

---

## 1. Buyer due diligence path

You are evaluating Razorglint technology for purchase, licensing, or integration.

### What to look at first

1. [PAINPOINT_INDEX.md](PAINPOINT_INDEX.md) — find your pain
2. [SYSTEM_SOLUTION_MAP.md](SYSTEM_SOLUTION_MAP.md) — find the system that solves it
3. [CLAIM_BOUNDARIES.md](CLAIM_BOUNDARIES.md) — understand what is and is not claimed

### Which system matters most

- **Prestige-Forge** — produces the proof bundles you would inspect
- **Truthpacks** — the portable format you would receive and verify

### Which painpoints it solves

- PP-006: Buyer cannot verify claims
- PP-013: A good system still needs a clear buying path
- PP-014: Technical proof does not automatically sell

### What proof you would inspect (elsewhere)

- `systems-architecture/EVIDENCE_MAP/` — benchmark evidence
- `systems-architecture/APPROVED_PUBLIC_CLAIMS.md` — 28 approved claims with evidence citations
- Each system repo — test suites (run `pytest` or `cargo test`)

### What decision it helps you make

"Is this technology real, and can I verify the claims myself without trusting the vendor?"

---

## 2. Internal operator path

You are running these systems inside your organization.

### What to look at first

1. [SYSTEM_SOLUTION_MAP.md](SYSTEM_SOLUTION_MAP.md) — understand what each system does
2. System files in `systems/` — practical usage patterns
3. [LICENSE_TIER_MAP.md](LICENSE_TIER_MAP.md) — confirm your tier includes what you need

### Which system matters most

- **SAR Guardian** — enforcement and receipts for daily operations
- **ChessClock** — temporal event trail for debugging
- **Persistent Operational Memory** — context that persists across sessions

### Which painpoints it solves

- PP-004: Operational memory gets lost
- PP-005: Runtime failure gets hidden
- PP-011: Operators cannot debug the system

### What proof you would inspect (elsewhere)

- Receipt chains in your local SAR deployment
- ChessClock event logs (append-only JSONL)
- Proof bundles generated by your Prestige-Forge instance

### What decision it helps you make

"Can I trace what happened, why it happened, and whether governance was enforced?"

## 3. Security / governance review path

You are a security team, auditor, or governance reviewer.

### What to look at first

1. [CLAIM_BOUNDARIES.md](CLAIM_BOUNDARIES.md) — what is safe to claim
2. [PAINPOINT_INDEX.md](PAINPOINT_INDEX.md) — find governance gaps
3. System files for SAR Guardian, Voidlock, Command Guardian

### Which system matters most

- **Voidlock** — execution cages with deny-by-default capabilities
- **SAR Guardian** — signed, hash-chained enforcement receipts
- **Claims Safety Engine** — prevents overclaiming

### Which painpoints it solves

- PP-001: Cannot prove what the AI did
- PP-002: Denied actions disappear
- PP-007: Fraud / control bypass
- PP-012: Compliance language can create legal risk

### What proof you would inspect (elsewhere)

- Voidlock receipt files (self-verifying SHA-256)
- SAR receipt chains (Ed25519 signed, hash-linked)
- `systems-architecture/FORBIDDEN_PUBLIC_CLAIMS.md` — what must not be said

### What decision it helps you make

"Is governance enforced with proof, or is it just policy documents?"
