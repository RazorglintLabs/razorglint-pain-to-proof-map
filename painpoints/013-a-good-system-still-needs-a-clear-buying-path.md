# A Good System Still Needs a Clear Buying Path

## The pain in one sentence

Even excellent technology sits unsold if the buyer cannot understand what it does, who it is for, what tier to buy, or how to evaluate it.

## Who feels this pain

- Buyer — confused by technical complexity
- Founder — has built something real but cannot sell it
- Investor — sees technology but no go-to-market clarity

## What this pain impacts

- **Money:** Great technology with no buying path generates zero revenue
- **Buyer confidence:** Confused buyers do not buy — they move on
- **Reputation:** Inability to explain your product looks like inability to build it

## What usually goes wrong

A team builds a strong governance system. It has real proof, real tests, real receipts. But the buyer lands on the website and sees technical jargon, no tier structure, no clear "this is what you get for this price," and no obvious way to start. The buyer evaluates three alternatives, picks the one that was easiest to understand, and moves on.

## How those systems solve it

This repo maps every pain to a system, every system to a tier, and every tier to what the buyer gets. Truthpacks give the buyer something concrete to evaluate. The LICENSE_TIER_MAP shows exactly what is included at each level. The buyer can go from "I have this problem" to "this system solves it" to "this tier includes it" to "here is the proof" in minutes.

This does not prove the technology is the best choice. It proves the buying path is clear and the claims are mapped to evidence. The buyer still needs to evaluate fit for their specific situation.
