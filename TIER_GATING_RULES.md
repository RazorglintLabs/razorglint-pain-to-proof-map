# Tier Gating Rules

How painpoint coverage, system depth, and proof access are gated by license tier.

---

## Core rule

Critical painpoints may be named in public material, but their full solution path, implementation depth, integration logic, or custom proof package must not be included in free or cheap pilots.

---

## Tier definitions

### Free / Public Proof Reference

- May explain pain in plain language
- May reference public maps, indexes, and glossary
- May see system names and one-sentence explanations
- **Does NOT include:** proof bundles, solution-path depth, integration logic, governed-agent access, receipt chains, custom Truthpacks, simulation access

### Starter

- Includes basic memory/benchmark understanding
- Limited verification access (read-only Truthpack samples)
- Basic governance explanation (PP-004, PP-007, PP-011, PP-013, PP-014)
- **Does NOT include:** SAR enforcement receipts, FleetSim trust-plane depth, OpenClaw governed agents, claim safety engine, custom proof packages, high-assurance evidence paths

### Professional

- Includes SAR Guardian enforcement receipts on covered paths
- Includes Truthpacks (full buyer-verifiable proof bundles)
- Includes Prestige-Forge claim safety review
- Includes Command Guardian governed terminal evidence
- Includes ChessClock temporal evidence
- Covers: PP-001, PP-002, PP-003, PP-005, PP-006, PP-008, PP-010, PP-012, PP-017, PP-018, PP-020, PP-022, PP-025
- **Does NOT include:** FleetSim simulation-grade trust-plane, OpenClaw full governed-agent integration, air-gapped verification packages, regulatory-readiness mapping, authority-level enforcement modeling

### Operator

- Includes FleetSim simulation-grade trust-plane modeling
- Includes CTLM Engine full memory
- Includes PolicySourceEngine policy drift visibility
- Includes authority-level modeling (A0–A5)
- Covers: PP-009, PP-016, PP-019, PP-021, PP-026
- **Does NOT include:** OpenClaw full governed-agent stack, EU AI Act regulatory-readiness mapping, air-gapped high-assurance evidence packages, BountyPipeline, custom enterprise proof bundles

### Enterprise

- Full stack governance review
- OpenClaw governed agents with SAR bridge
- BountyPipeline security evidence packaging
- EU AI Act regulatory-readiness evidence mapping
- Air-gapped verification packages
- High-assurance chain-of-custody evidence paths
- Covers: PP-015, PP-023, PP-024 + all lower-tier painpoints at full depth
- Custom proof packages, integration support, and Truthpack tailoring

### OEM / White-label

- Negotiated embedding of selected modules into buyer's platform
- Per-module licensing with capability boundaries
- Integration support and white-label rights

### Strategic License

- Negotiated exclusivity for vertical, geography, or use case
- Selected subset or full stack
- Competitive differentiation rights

### Private IP Sale / Acquisition

- Negotiated ownership transfer
- Full source + IP rights
- Due diligence package at Enterprise depth

---

## Gating enforcement principles

1. **Naming is free, depth is gated.** Any painpoint may be named publicly. Solution depth requires tier access.
2. **Pilots show capability, not full integration.** A pilot demonstrates governed behavior on a scoped path — it does not deliver production integration.
3. **Upgrade path is always visible.** Every tier boundary shows the buyer what the next tier unlocks.
4. **No reverse gating.** Once a buyer has tier access, they keep it for the license duration.
5. **S1 Critical painpoints require Professional or above** for any solution-path access.
6. **Enterprise-only painpoints (PP-015, PP-023, PP-024) are never included in lower-tier pilots** without explicit upgrade agreement.
