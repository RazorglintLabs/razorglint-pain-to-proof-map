# Claim Boundaries — Current Public Wording

**Reviewed 16 October 2026.** This file supersedes the former generic list for new outreach. It does **not** claim a new automated Prestige-Reforged scan of these changes.

## Use these bounded statements

- “Compliance Drift Detector analyses **operator-supplied structured policies and pre-classified observations** across checkpoints; current controlled Windows evaluation has a [dated 76-test qualification receipt](https://github.com/RazorglintLabs/ComplianceDriftDetector/blob/main/docs/RELEASE_STATUS.md).”
- “Prestige-Reforged is being developed as a source-bound evidence/claim verification system; desktop product delivery is under separate review.”
- “SAR uses an explicit governance boundary in its tested scope.”
- “VEAC challenges specific campaign scopes independently; a CLEAR result does not establish overall safety.”
- “The planned GCA Stack remains in source/IP and component-admission review.”
- “Legacy April portfolio counts are historical and include recorded environment-related limits.”
- “Cryptographic **integrity** does not authenticate a source, establish truth or replace an audit.”

## Do not imply

- Certification, EU AI Act compliance, legal sufficiency, production hardening or zero possible bypasses.
- A current customer deployment, market demand, cash savings, contract, source transfer or valuation without independent evidence.
- That all systems are separately deployed, open source, available to clone, signed, autonomous or mutually integrated.
- That the May 12 `claim_surface_auditor` PASS covers this October update.
- That a hash-only artifact is digitally signed or authenticated.
- That individual legacy test totals add up to a current comparable portfolio total.

## Release decision

A public statement about **current** software must link to the particular project's exact commit/release/CI/test record, state platform and limitations, and respect proprietary rights. If those facts are unavailable, describe the asset as **documented engineering**, **historical proof** or **candidate integration** instead.
